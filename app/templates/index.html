Book & Record

Create a Calendly-style booking slot, share the link, then paste a transcript or upload audio. The app stores the meeting, runs a structured prompt, and shows a summary, checkpoints, decisions and a to-do list with owners.

Stack: FastAPI · JWT cookie auth · SQLite · Whisper (or demo) · JSON-constrained LLM prompt

Review Rewards

A restaurant owner adds branches, each with a public review link and QR code. A guest submits a review; the app scores sentiment, tags food / service / wait, issues a coupon or cashback code, and the owner dashboard reads it all back from the database.

Stack: FastAPI · JWT · SQLite · QR codes · sentiment prompt (or keyword demo) · coupon ledger
